Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What ages and grade levels do you support? +

We work with students from Pre-K through 12th grade, including learning assessments, homework help, and standardized test preparation.

What kind of assessments do you offer? +

We offer academic and skill-based assessments and diagnostics used to build an individualized action plan for each learner, with ongoing progress tracking shared with caregivers.

Do you support homeschool families? +

Yes. We offer dedicated homeschool support, including curriculum guidance, skill-building plans, and enrichment sessions, and we partner with homeschool groups like Williams Academy of Excellence.

Do you offer virtual, in-person, and mobile sessions? +

Yes. We offer live virtual sessions over Zoom available anywhere, private-office sessions in Winter Springs, Florida, and mobile sessions where we come to you.

Do you work with neurodivergent students? +

Yes. Neurodivergent students are our specialty; our approach blends assessments, individualized tutoring, and homeschool support tailored to each learner.

How do we get started? +

Reach out through our contact form below or through our social channels to schedule a free introductory conversation about your child's needs.
