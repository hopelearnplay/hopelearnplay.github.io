Who we are

Hello, I'm Monique Harris - known as Ms. Monique to my students

Hope, Learn & Play LLC is an educational support business built on compassion, creativity, and a commitment to the whole child. We specialize in neurodivergent students, offering assessments, individualized tutoring, and homeschool support for families across Central Florida and beyond.

Our story

Being a working parent or caregiver isn't easy

Many families are caught between the demands of work and the responsibility of caring for their children: a challenge I understand deeply, because I've lived it.

2014 · Sterling Heights, Michigan

After the birth of my first child, I left my corporate management role and opened a Licensed Home Daycare through the State of Michigan. Hope, Learn & Play was born.

Move to Florida

I became a full-time preschool teacher, worked with Florida VPK programs, and facilitated after-school enrichment across Central Florida.

Orlando Science Center

I created and led the Virtual Preschool Connection Program, helping families navigate remote learning during the pandemic, an experience that deepened my commitment to supporting children and their families through changing times.

Today · Winter Springs, Florida

Hope, Learn & Play LLC is a full-service educational support business specializing in neurodivergent learners, offering assessments, individualized tutoring, and homeschool support for families across Central Florida and beyond, in-home, virtual, and on-the-go.

Our approach

Meeting families where they are

I've dedicated my life to helping children build not just academic skills, but confidence, emotional awareness, and a genuine love for learning. What the past few years have taught us is that learning doesn't have to look the same for every child, and that caregivers deserve support, too.

At Hope, Learn & Play, our mission is to provide the academic and skill-building tools your child needs to thrive, while giving you, the caregiver, the encouragement and resources to feel empowered along the way. We proudly partner with schools, homeschool groups like Williams Academy of Excellence, and organizations looking to offer engaging, inclusive learning experiences.

Sincerely, Ms. Monique
